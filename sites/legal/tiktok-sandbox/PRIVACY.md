# Ifverse TikTok sandbox privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026.

This notice covers the TikTok connection in the private Ifverse Social Account Manager sandbox.

**Information used.** When you authorize the connection, Ifverse receives the TikTok account identifier and display name, plus the username if TikTok provides it with the approved permissions. Ifverse uses that information to show which TikTok account is connected and verify its identity.

**Connection storage.** Ifverse saves the connection under your signed-in Ifverse user. It stores the OAuth access and refresh tokens encrypted with the account record and uses them to verify or refresh the connection with TikTok. TikTok handles the TikTok sign-in.

**Your choices.** You can decline on TikTok's consent screen. You can use **Remove** next to the connection in the Ifverse dashboard to delete its saved record. You can also revoke the app's access in TikTok's connected-app settings.

For questions about your connection or data, contact the Ifverse operator who granted your sandbox access.
