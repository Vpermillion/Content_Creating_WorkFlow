# Ifverse TikTok sandbox terms of service

Effective November 21, 2026.

The Ifverse Social Account Manager is a private testing tool for the Ifverse operator and invited testers. The sandbox lets an eligible tester connect a TikTok account they own or are authorized to manage and check that Ifverse identifies the right profile.

- Use the sandbox only with your own account or an account you have permission to manage.
- Use the TikTok consent screen to choose whether to grant the requested permissions. TikTok's terms also apply when you use TikTok.
- The sandbox connection is for profile verification. TikTok publishing is completed separately in TikTok Studio.
- You can stop using the sandbox and remove your connection from the account dashboard at any time.

For questions about using the sandbox, contact the Ifverse operator who granted your access.
