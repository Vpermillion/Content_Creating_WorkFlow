# What if Shikamaru had ONE day to fix Konoha?

What if Shikamaru got one day to fix Konoha? Bro would defeat the village's strongest villain. Administrative incompetence.

In our alternate scenario, he inherits a desk buried in mission requests. Naruto calls this leadership. My guy, that's composting.

Shikamaru doesn't invent a bigger jutsu. He asks why three teams keep arriving at the same gate together every morning.

Because nobody coordinates departures. Excellent. An entire ninja village got outplayed by a calendar with trust issues and no owner.

He staggers departures, groups nearby deliveries, and leaves a reserve team. Suddenly emergencies stop borrowing people from other emergencies.

Then he checks the hospital supply requests. Medicine is waiting for approval from somebody currently waiting for medicine. Beautiful circular stupidity.

A clear supply route fixes the delay. No dramatic explosion. Just fewer injured people waiting while paperwork auditions for main character.

Someone complains that the improvements look boring. Motherflicker, the objective is a functioning village, not a trailer with six beat drops.

But planning alone won't hear every problem. Shikamaru has to listen to civilians who don't phrase their lives like tactical puzzles.

He changes the plan after they speak. That matters more than pretending the smartest guy automatically knows everybody's needs already.

By afternoon, the village runs smoother. Naturally, he uses the saved time to take a nap. Finally, a coherent political platform.

Naruto asks for his secret technique. Shikamaru hands him a schedule. The village trained assassins for decades and forgot appointments exist.
