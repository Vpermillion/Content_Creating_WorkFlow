# What if Light had to explain EVERY name to L?

What if Light had to explain every name to L before the Death Note worked? Bro just discovered mandatory peer review.

This is an invented notebook rule. Every entry pauses until Light gives a reason. L gets the explanation and can challenge it.

Light says the person looks guilty. L asks for evidence. My guy, cheekbone analysis is not a functioning criminal justice system.

Then Light says everybody online agrees. L asks where the original report came from. Suddenly the god complex needs a bibliography.

Ryuk is entertained. Humans acquired a supernatural notebook and somehow recreated the worst group project at university. Pass the apples, please.

Light tries a longer speech. The problem is still missing evidence. Adding dramatic breathing does not upgrade a guess into a fact.

L starts comparing the explanations. Which cases get certainty? Which deserve patience? Why does criticism suddenly become a suspicious personality trait?

That's where the scheme cracks. Light's idea of justice keeps bending around his own ego. Very convenient weather system he's running there.

The notebook doesn't magically make L correct either. Investigations still need evidence, checks, and the possibility that both geniuses missed something.

The battle becomes who can defend a conclusion, not who can deliver the smuggest indoor monologue while holding a pen dramatically.

Ryuk asks whether they plan to finish today. L requests supporting documents. Congratulations, the supernatural horror has evolved into an academic hearing.

Light wanted unquestioned authority. Instead he gets follow-up questions. His greatest enemy isn't another notebook. It's somebody calmly saying, source, bro?
