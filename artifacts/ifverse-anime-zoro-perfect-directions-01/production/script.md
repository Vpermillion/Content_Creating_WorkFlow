# What if Zoro finally learned directions?

What if Zoro finally learned directions? Somewhere, a map just felt the unfamiliar sensation of being respected by that man.

Nami tests him in this imaginary timeline. Left means left. Incredible. The man has unlocked a feature available on most shopping carts.

Zoro reaches the meeting point first. Then the enemy base. Then the actual problem. Nobody else is there to back him up.

Turns out being early doesn't make you invincible. He's standing inside an ambush with three swords and absolutely no emotional support.

Luffy is still somewhere causing property damage. We fixed one man's navigation, not the entire crew's long-running relationship with basic planning.

Zoro spots extra guards and hidden exits. Old Zoro might charge. This version knows exactly how far away his friends are.

So he retreats and marks the approach. My guy, that's not cowardice. That's character development wearing earrings and refusing eye contact.

He reports the ambush to Nami. They choose a better rendezvous and arrive together. The enemies lose their easiest advantage for free.

Now he fights with cover instead of auditioning to become a cautionary flashback. Reliable timing is carrying more weight than the compass.

Knowing the route doesn't erase his stubbornness. He still argues with the plan. At least his complaints arrive at the correct address.

Nami buys him a tiny compass anyway. Zoro asks if it comes in sword form. Of course. Bro needs navigation with a damage stat.

The crew finally travels in one direction. Luffy points toward the wrong island. Excellent. The navigation disease has found a new host.
