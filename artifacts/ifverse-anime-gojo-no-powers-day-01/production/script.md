# What if Gojo lost his powers for 24 hours?

What if Gojo lost his powers for twenty-four hours? The blindfold stays. The confidence stays. The backup plan has left the building.

For this hypothetical, his technique is unavailable for one day. No convenient secret upgrade. We are making the teacher actually use the syllabus.

Yuji asks whether they should hide him. Gojo suggests making it a lesson. My guy, everything is a lesson when you're avoiding embarrassment.

Megumi asks the useful question: who knows Gojo is vulnerable? Because announcing the challenge publicly would be a beautifully stupid marketing campaign.

Gojo still has experience, knowledge, and judgment. Losing a technique doesn't delete his brain. We are not doing a personality factory reset.

So the team changes its objective. Get people out, limit exposure, and avoid a direct fight. Winning today means reaching tomorrow intact.

Yuji wants to rescue everyone himself. Gojo makes him coordinate. Strong instincts are useful. Running alone into every problem is free delivery service.

A threat appears, and Gojo can't solve it with his normal display of force. The team uses a decoy and a planned escape.

Megumi handles the route. Yuji helps people move. Gojo calls timing. Suddenly the students aren't an audience waiting for the strongest guy's entrance.

That's the uncomfortable part for him. He has to trust what he taught. Incredible. Education finally reaches its terrifying practical application today.

The powers return tomorrow in our scenario. But the lesson survives longer: a team shouldn't collapse because its strongest member has a bad day.

Gojo calls it an intentional exercise. Megumi asks for the lesson plan. Brother, the receipts have entered the domain and they are undefeated.
