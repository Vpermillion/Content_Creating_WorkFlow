# What if Tanjiro could hear demons' thoughts?

What if Tanjiro could hear demons' thoughts? The universe added notifications with no mute button available.

This is an invented ability. Understanding suffering might help him protect people.

Instead, fear and memories arrive together. Welcome to a cursed open-plan office. Terrible acoustics.

Zenitsu asks if it helps, screams. Outstanding. His own emergency broadcast system.

A demon thinks it means no harm. Reassuring, except hearing a thought doesn't make it reliable.

Frightened people can hurt others. Confident memories can mislead. Empathy still needs evidence and practical boundaries.

Nezuko reminds him why he cares. Someone can deserve compassion without everybody else surrendering their safety.

He protects bystanders first, then listens. He installed a firewall on that enormous heart.

A hidden memory suggests a missing person's location. He checks instead of trusting blindly. The ability helps.

Zenitsu asks if Tanjiro can mute him. Different species, brother. Your volume is a local government issue.

Understanding doesn't promise agreement or painless solutions. The new ability changes his choices, not every consequence outside.

Tanjiro keeps his kindness and learns restraint. The upgrade wasn't hearing everything. It was knowing when to listen.
