# What if Tanjiro could hear demons' thoughts?

What if Tanjiro could hear demons' thoughts? Bro already smells emotions. Now the universe added notifications with absolutely no mute button available.

This is our invented ability, not canon. He hopes it will help him understand suffering and protect people without guessing what they're hiding.

The first problem is noise. Fear, hunger, memories, and lies all compete at once. Welcome to the world's most cursed open-plan office.

Zenitsu asks whether hearing thoughts is useful. Then screams before anybody answers. Outstanding. The man has brought his own emergency broadcast system again.

Tanjiro hears a demon thinking it means no harm. That sounds reassuring. Except hearing a thought doesn't make the thought honest or reliable.

A frightened person can still hurt somebody. A confident memory can still be wrong. Compassion is not a substitute for checking what's happening.

Nezuko reminds him why he cares. There can be a person inside the suffering. That matters without magically removing everybody else's right to safety.

So he protects the bystanders first, then listens. Boundaries before the emotional speech. My guy finally installed a firewall on his enormous heart.

A half-hidden memory points toward a missing person. He checks the location instead of trusting blindly. Now the new power actually helps somebody.

Zenitsu wants to know if Tanjiro can mute him too. Different species, brother. Unfortunately your volume problem remains a strictly local government issue.

Some encounters still end badly. Understanding doesn't promise agreement or a painless solution. The ability changes his choices, not every consequence waiting outside.

Tanjiro keeps his kindness and learns restraint. The strongest upgrade wasn't hearing everything. It was deciding what deserved an answer, a boundary, or silence.
