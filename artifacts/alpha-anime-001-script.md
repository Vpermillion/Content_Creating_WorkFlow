# What if Naruto joined the Akatsuki?

PROVISIONAL DEMONSTRATION — REVIEW REQUIRED

What if Naruto joined the Akatsuki? The first surprise is that joining would not make him safe.

He carries the Nine Tails, and the Akatsuki wants the tailed beasts. Their new recruit is also their target.

In this alternate timeline, Naruto offers a deal: his help, in exchange for protecting his friends.

At first, the missions give him the respect he always wanted. But every favor comes with a price.

The breaking point arrives when his new allies demand that he turn against the Hidden Leaf.

He refuses. Now both sides question his loyalty, and the deal that protected his friends falls apart.

Naruto has to choose: prove himself to the organization, or protect the people who still believe in him.

Could he change the Akatsuki from inside? Or would joining them change Naruto first?
