# What if Vegeta had one peaceful week?

What if Vegeta got one peaceful week? No invasion. No tournament. Just a prince violently underqualified for a normal Tuesday afternoon.

In this alternate little story, he starts by training harder. Naturally. Bro treats relaxation like a security vulnerability in his operating system.

Bulma asks him to spend one afternoon with his family. He asks what the objective is. Sir, the objective is being present.

Trunks wants to show him something. Vegeta starts correcting his stance. My guy, the kid brought a drawing, not an enemy combatant.

Without a threat, Vegeta faces an annoying question. What is all that strength for if he can't enjoy the people it protects?

Then Goku visits. Vegeta immediately suspects secret training. This man hears a friendly hello and opens an imaginary competitive investigation on instinct.

He's tempted to disappear into the gravity room again. Instead he agrees to a short session, then actually leaves when he promised.

Bulma is shocked. Not by a transformation. By a grown man correctly estimating his own arrival time. Historic scenes at Capsule Corporation.

He listens to Trunks explain the drawing. It's their family. Nobody is fighting. The prince doesn't know what to say at first.

That doesn't turn him into a completely different person. He keeps the pride. He just stops using it to dodge every tender moment.

Goku asks if peace made him soft. Vegeta says he can schedule the beating for tomorrow. Healthy boundaries. Horrible phrasing. Real progress.

The week ends without a new form. He still grows. Motherflicker, the breakthrough was dinner on time and one sincere compliment delivered at last.
