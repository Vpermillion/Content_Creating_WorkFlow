# Standalone YouTube connection

For a requested local/standalone connection, follow [YOUTUBE.md](../docs/YOUTUBE.md) on a supported runtime with a browser-reachable loopback callback. Choose the read-only or upload utility according to the task and verify the exact channel ID after consent.

For normal ChatGPT/browser use, follow [hosted account linking](../sites/ACCOUNT_LINKING.md) instead. Keep credential stores separate and report only non-secret status.
