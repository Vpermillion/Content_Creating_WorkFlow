# Original Ifverse episode brief

This is our internal planning template, not an official YouTube checklist or monetization certificate. Read `docs/MONETIZATION_REVIEW.md`, `docs/POLICY_FACT_CHECK.md`, `prompts/script-style.md` and CURRENT. Keep the preferred `am_michael` voice and the funny v2 persona unless the owner changes them.

- **Episode premise / point of view:** What specific alternate scenario or interpretation are we creating? What will the viewer get besides seeing familiar clips?
- **Original development:** Outline the setup, causal consequences, reversal and payoff. Explain the episode's own reasoning or comic construction. Avoid a template where only names and footage change.
- **Canon / invention:** List factual premises and research sources where relevant; clearly label the counterfactual events and invented dialogue. Do not pass fan fiction off as a leaked/official scene.
- **Difference from recent episodes:** Identify meaningful differences in premise, argument, story construction and visual explanation. Recurring branding and voice can stay consistent.
- **Visual explanation:** For each beat, specify the concrete entity/action/place or original diagram/comparison that helps the viewer understand it. Keep source-specific reasons and provenance.
- **Source-use evidence:** For each asset, record ownership/license/permission or a genuinely reviewed exception rationale with evidence and restrictions. A public URL, checksum, short duration or another channel's use is not that evidence. Resolve unknown rights before publication clearance; do not invent permission.
- **Sound design:** Choose effects for a reason, audition their actual content and check voice intelligibility. Community recordings are not assumed cleared. Use documented original/licensed alternatives when appropriate and keep any creative replacement explicit.
- **Presentation:** Write the full new script in the selected roast style. Prepare an accurate title/thumbnail/description, contextual ad-suitability review and an altered/synthetic-disclosure decision where applicable. No slur or strong profanity is required to make a joke work.
- **Audience:** Assess intended audience and the actual topic, language, characters, presentation and other relevant factors. Animation alone does not establish made-for-kids status; record the basis rather than defaulting all anime either way.
- **Review evidence:** Record who actually reviewed what, which unresolved issues remain, the final source/script/audio/video hashes and the decision. Leave unsigned/unknown fields pending. Code success is not editorial approval.
- **Publication scope:** Verify actual channel eligibility and connection only when needed, then obtain/confirm the specific authorized publishing scope. This brief alone authorizes no upload or monetization claim.
