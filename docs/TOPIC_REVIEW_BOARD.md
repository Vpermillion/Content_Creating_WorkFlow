## Stronger proposed forks — owner choice pending

1. **Kakashi reaches the Valley before Naruto and Sasuke's final clash.** Timeline: end of Part I, after the retrieval squad splits up. Fork: Kakashi changes his pursuit route and arrives while both boys can still disengage. Existing tools: tracking, speed, Sharingan and Chidori; he cannot teleport, use later Kamui mastery or undo the curse mark. Cost: intercepting risks injury and lets other threats escape. Consequence: he may interrupt the duel and return Sasuke under supervision, but Sasuke's resentment and Orochimaru's influence remain. Earlier arrival is the stated AU timing change, not a hidden new power. No guaranteed permanent reconciliation.
2. **Itachi tells Sasuke the truth before their final fight.** Timeline: their final confrontation, before the fatal fight resolves. Fork: Itachi chooses disclosure instead of performing the villain role to the end. Existing limits: his illness, failing vision, Sasuke's distrust and the danger posed by Obito and village leadership. Disclosure is testimony, not instant proof or mind control; memories/genjutsu may be doubted. Cost: abandoning the staged outcome risks his plan and Sasuke's safety. Consequence: Sasuke may pause to investigate rather than immediately forgive Konoha; neither brother becomes suddenly healthy. AU mechanism: a changed decision to speak, no new jutsu.
3. **Jiraiya retreats from Pain with incomplete intelligence.** Timeline: the Rain infiltration, after encountering multiple bodies but before the final attempt to verify their secret makes escape impossible. Fork: prioritize withdrawal with Fukasaku's help while a route remains. Existing tools: summons, barriers and sage techniques; no late teleport/resurrection rescue. Cost: withdrawal is dangerous and he leaves without the full answer about Pain's bodies. Consequence: Konoha gains an experienced defender and partial warning, yet Nagato can adapt and the invasion remains dangerous. Survival and a prevented invasion are possible outcomes to argue, not guaranteed rewards. AU mechanism: an earlier retreat decision.

No chapter/episode citations are asserted. Verify exact scene chronology from legitimate canon sources during the chosen outline review. Compare these forks semantically with all taken topics, not just their titles.
